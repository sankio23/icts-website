The Invitation

Join ICTS.

The Center welcomes universities, research centers and institutions that share its purpose — and individual researchers whose questions meet its own.

Why partner with ICTS

Five characteristics of the Center's institutional design.

  • 01

    A defined epistemological position

    ICTS does not call generically for interdisciplinarity. It advances a specific thesis about relational objects of inquiry — developed in its researchers' own published work — and organises its research structures around that thesis. Partners engage with a position, not with an aspiration.

  • 02

    An operating structure already in place

    From its founding year the Center comprises three Research Units, four Research Groups and three Strategic Initiatives, with constituted governance across four distinct bodies and a network of partner institutions on five continents.

  • 03

    Questions that matter, held together

    Human agency under artificial intelligence, the foundations of information, democratic cohesion, peace and mediation, integral care. Most institutions address one of these questions. ICTS is structured to hold them in relation.

  • 04

    Intercontinental by construction

    Two founding universities in Europe and Latin America, and partner institutions in ten countries on five continents — from the founding campuses in Tuscany and Pernambuco to partners in North America, Asia and Africa.

  • 05

    Research that produces instruments

    Alongside scholarly publication, the Center produces frameworks, protocols, forums and training programmes designed to be used by institutions, professionals and policymakers.

Ways to join

Two ways in.

Researchers join individually, through affiliation to a Research Unit. Institutions join through an agreement with Sophia University Institute, which hosts the administrative headquarters of the Center. Both routes are deliberated by the Council Board.

  • 01

    Individual researchers

    Professors, researchers and scholars of established competence apply for affiliation to one of the Center's Research Units. Applicants from institutions already in agreement with ICTS are deliberated directly by the Council Board; applicants from elsewhere are first endorsed by the coordinator of the Unit concerned. Members take part in the Unit's research programme, its seminars and its publications.

    Apply for individual membership (Model A) →

  • 02

    Institutions

    Universities, research centers and institutions enter the Center through a Memorandum of Understanding with Sophia University Institute. Researchers of a partner institution may then join existing Research Units and Groups, and the institution may propose the constitution of a new Research Group. The standard terms are set out in full on the application page, so that they can be considered before any commitment; signature follows deliberation of the Council Board.

For members of the Center

  • Model C · New laboratory or research group

    Laboratories and research groups are the Center’s lighter instruments: thematic spaces, physical or virtual, pursuing a specific line of work. They sit outside the research units and across them, and colleagues from outside the Center may take part in one without being members. Any member may propose one; the Council Board constitutes it, and may in time constitute an established group as a research unit. Once a proposal is registered you receive a reference and a link, so that each person named can send a short letter of participation (Model D).
