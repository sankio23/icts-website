The Invitation

Join ICTS.

There are three ways into the Center. What separates them is not how committed you are, but what you are bringing: yourself, your institution, or a piece of work and the people to do it with.

  • You.

    Model A · Individual membership

    Professors, researchers and scholars of established competence are affiliated to one of the Center’s Research Units, and take part in its research programme, its seminars and its publications. If your institution already holds an agreement with ICTS, the Council Board deliberates directly; otherwise the coordinator of the Unit concerned endorses the application first.

    Apply for membership →

      After you apply

    1. 01You send the form.
    2. 02The coordinator of the Unit endorses it, unless your institution already holds an agreement.
    3. 03The Council Board deliberates.
    4. 04You hear the outcome by email.

  • Your institution.

    Model B · Institutional participation

    Universities, research centers and institutions enter the Center through an agreement with Sophia University Institute, which hosts its administrative headquarters. What you send is an expression of interest, not a commitment: it opens a conversation. We reply with the Regulations and a draft of the agreement, so that the people at your institution who decide these things can read the terms before anything is decided. Once the agreement is in place, researchers of your institution may join the Center’s existing units and groups.

    Send an expression of interest →

      After you write

    1. 01You send the expression of interest.
    2. 02We reply with the Regulations and a draft of the agreement.
    3. 03We talk, and your institution takes its own time.
    4. 04The Council Board deliberates; signature follows.

  • A piece of work.

    Model C · Laboratory or research group

    Laboratories and research groups are the Center’s lighter instruments: a thematic space, physical or virtual, on one specific line of work. They sit outside the research units and across them. You do not need to be a member to propose one — what you need is at least one other person willing to do the work with you. If the Council Board constitutes it, the coordinator becomes a member of the Center by that act, and an established group may in time be constituted as a research unit.

    Propose a laboratory or group →

      After you propose

    1. 01You send the proposal.
    2. 02You receive a reference and a link to pass on.
    3. 03Each person named sends a short letter of participation.
    4. 04The Council Board deliberates.

Before you start

All three are deliberated by the Council Board of the Center, under the Regulations. Nothing you send binds you, and none of it is published: we read it, we write back, and the decision is taken by the Council. If you would rather ask something first, write to icts@sophiauniversity.org.

If a coordinator has asked you to confirm that you will take part in a laboratory, they will have sent you a link. Write to us if you no longer have it.
