The Invitation

Join ICTS.

The Center welcomes dialogue with universities, foundations, public institutions and research communities — and with individual researchers whose questions meet its own.

Why partner with ICTS

Five characteristics of the Center's institutional design.

  • 01

    A defined epistemological position

    ICTS does not call generically for interdisciplinarity. It advances a specific thesis about relational objects of inquiry — developed in its researchers' own published work — and organises its research structures around that thesis. Partners engage with a position, not with an aspiration.

  • 02

    An operating structure already in place

    From its founding year the Center comprises three Research Units, four Research Groups and three Strategic Initiatives, with constituted governance across four distinct bodies and a network of partner institutions on five continents.

  • 03

    Questions that matter, held together

    Human agency under artificial intelligence, the foundations of information, democratic cohesion, peace and mediation, integral care. Most institutions address one of these questions. ICTS is structured to hold them in relation.

  • 04

    Intercontinental by construction

    Two founding universities in Europe and Latin America, and partner institutions in ten countries on five continents — from the founding campuses in Tuscany and Pernambuco to partners in North America, Asia and Africa.

  • 05

    Research that produces instruments

    Alongside scholarly publication, the Center produces frameworks, protocols, forums and training programmes designed to be used by institutions, professionals and policymakers.

Ways to engage

Four forms of engagement.

From academic association to structural support for a Research Unit. Each form of engagement is defined jointly with the Council Board and, where research content is concerned, evaluated by the Scientific Committee. The terms, duration and visibility of a partnership are established in a written agreement.

  • 01

    Institutional Partnership

    A formal agreement between ICTS and a university, research center or institution. Partners host or co-host research activities, participate in joint projects and funding applications, and take part in the Center's programme of seminars, conferences and doctoral formation. This is the standard form of academic association with the Center.

    Request a Memorandum of Understanding (Model B) →

  • 02

    Research Chair

    The endowment of a named position within one of the Research Units, dedicated to a defined line of inquiry. A Chair carries the name of the supporting institution or benefactor and is accompanied by a programme of publication, convening and supervision agreed with the Unit and approved by the Scientific Committee.

    Write to the Center →

  • 03

    Unit Endowment

    Structural support for a Research Unit or Group over a multi-year horizon, covering its research programme, its seminars and forums, and the postdoctoral positions attached to it. Unit endowment is the form of engagement with the greatest effect on what the Center is able to undertake.

    Propose a Research Group or Unit (Model C) →

  • 04

    Fellowship Programme

    Support for doctoral and postdoctoral researchers working across the Center's Units, including research mobility between founding and partner institutions in Europe and Latin America. Fellowships may be thematic, institutional or named.

    Apply for individual membership (Model A) →

Application forms

Begin a conversation.

Four instruments cover the forms of association currently open. Each is reviewed by the Coordination and, where relevant, submitted to the Council Board.

  • Model A · Individual membership

    For researchers who wish to be associated with a Research Unit or Group of the Center.

  • Model B · Institutional partnership

    For universities, research centers, foundations and institutions requesting a Memorandum of Understanding.

  • Model C · New Research Group or Unit

    For teams proposing the constitution of a new research structure within the Center.

  • Model D · Membership of a Research Group

    For researchers joining an already constituted Research Group.
