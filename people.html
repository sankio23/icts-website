People

The researchers of the Center.

ICTS brings together researchers from universities and institutions on five continents. Each belongs to one or more Research Units and Research Groups, working across philosophy, physics, history, political science, law, theology, computer science and the health sciences. The composition below reflects the 2026 founding edition of the Research Handbook.
