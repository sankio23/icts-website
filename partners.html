The Architecture

Institutions belong to ICTS in three distinct capacities.

Each capacity is defined by a different relation to the Center and to its governance. Institutional adherence is a separate matter from the membership of individual researchers in a Research Unit, and the two are decided by different bodies.

First capacity

Founding Members

The two universities that established the Center by framework agreement, whose governing bodies approved its Regulations and which compose the Council of the Center in its first term. They host, respectively, the administrative headquarters and the Research Groups of the Center.

Sophia University Institute

Loppiano, Figline e Incisa Valdarno · Italy

Established by the Holy See in 2007 at the initiative of Chiara Lubich, founder of the Focolare Movement, and opened in 2008 near Florence. It hosts the administrative headquarters of ICTS.

Tabosa de Almeida University Center, ASCES-UNITA

Caruaru, Pernambuco · Brazil

Rooted in the Associação Caruaruense de Ensino Superior, founded in 1959 in the Agreste of Pernambuco, it unites law, the health sciences and the social sciences. It hosts Research Groups of the Center.

Second capacity

Affiliated Universities

Universities that adhere to the Center by deliberation of their own governing bodies and take part in its scientific life as host or partner institutions of a Research Unit or Research Group. Adherence is admitted by a two-thirds majority of the Council and does not constitute a separate legal entity.

University of Perugia

Perugia, Umbria · Italy

Instituted in 1308 by bull of Clement V and among the oldest universities in Europe. It is a partner institution of RAISE, on artificial intelligence and ethics, and of CeC, on Christianity and culture.

Universidad de La Sabana

Chía, Cundinamarca · Colombia

Established in 1979 north of Bogotá, a private university of Christian inspiration. It hosts the coordination of PDM, the Research Group on peace, diplomacy and conflict mediation.

Nanyang Technological University

Singapore

Inaugurated in 1991 on the campus of the former Nanyang University, an autonomous public research university. It joins the Center on the epistemology of information and complexity, and on artificial intelligence in non-Western traditions.

Third capacity

Strategic Partners

Foundations, enterprises and civil society organisations that contribute to defined programmes of the Center — through funding, expertise, networks or resources in kind — under a memorandum of understanding. They hold no role in the governance of the Center.

Ethos Capital

Boston · United States

An investment firm in internet infrastructure and digital trust, founded by Fadi Chehadé, former chief executive of ICANN. A memorandum of understanding signed in March 2024 supports the Digital Oath.

Consulus

Singapore

An international consulting and innovation firm working on purpose-driven transformation. It collaborates with the Center on holistic leadership education and on the measurement of relational goods.

Focolare Foundation

Washington, DC · United States

A non-profit foundation constituted in 2024 to sustain the long-term mission of the Focolare Movement worldwide. It accompanies the Center's programmes in integral human development.

AI Oath Institute

Los Angeles · United States

An organisation devoted to the formulation and public adoption of professional commitments for those who design and deploy artificial intelligence, and a partner of the Digital Oath.

Affiliation and membership

Institutional affiliation and individual membership are distinct, and are decided by different bodies. A researcher may belong to a Research Unit of the Center without their home institution being affiliated; conversely, the adherence of an institution does not enrol its members in its Units.

Institutions wishing to adhere, and researchers wishing to join a Unit, follow different procedures — both set out on the Join page.
