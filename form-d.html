Model D

Letter of Participation.

Someone has proposed a laboratory or research group and has named you among the people who would take part. This short form is your letter of participation: the coordinator gathers them and presents them to the Council Board together with the proposal (Art. 10 of the Regulations). You do not need to be a member of the Center to take part.

Nothing here commits you. The proposal is still a draft and the Council Board may approve, decline or defer it. Signing this letter says you are willing to take part if it goes ahead — it creates no obligation and, on its own, no membership of the Center.

1 · The proposal

The code in the coordinator’s message, in the form LAB-2026-007. If you followed their link it is already filled in.
Only helps us match your letter if the code is mistyped.

2 · You

One or two lines. Useful to the Council Board, not required.

3 · Declarations

Knowledge emerges in relations — between persons, between levels of reality, between visions of the world.
