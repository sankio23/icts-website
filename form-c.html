Model C

New Laboratory or Research Group.

Laboratories and research groups are the Center’s lighter instruments: thematic spaces, physical or virtual, that pursue a specific line of work. They sit outside the research units and across them, and a group that establishes itself may in time be constituted as a unit by the Council Board. You do not need to be a member of the Center to propose one — what you need is at least one other person willing to do the work with you (Art. 10 of the Regulations).

This is a proposal, not a constitution. Everything you write here is provisional and is read by the Council Board, which alone constitutes laboratories and research groups. The name, the scope and the people involved can all still change. Ask for what you would like to do; the details are settled afterwards, together.
