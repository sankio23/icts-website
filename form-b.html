Model B

Institutional Agreement.

An expression of interest in bringing your institution into the Center. It commits no one: it opens a conversation. The standard terms of the agreement are set out below once you send it, so that you can take them to the office that handles agreements at your institution.

1 · You

This form is normally completed by the researcher who wishes to bring their institution into the Center, not by its legal representative.

2 · Your institution

3 · Where the proposal stands

There is no wrong answer. It tells us whether we are at the beginning of a conversation or close to a formal step.
Optional. We will not write to them without telling you first.

4 · The cooperation

What you would want the collaboration to be about: shared research interests, questions, activities. This is what the Council Board reads.
A framework convention is a general umbrella agreement that opens the relationship and under which specific activities are agreed later; a scientific collaboration agreement covers defined joint research on an identified subject; a researcher / faculty exchange agreement regulates the mobility of academic staff between the two institutions; a joint doctoral supervision agreement covers the co-supervision of doctoral candidates.
If more than one applies, or you are not sure yet, choose All of the above — nothing here is binding and the form is settled during the discussions that follow.

5 · Declarations

Knowledge emerges in relations — between persons, between levels of reality, between visions of the world.
