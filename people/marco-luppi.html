Coordinator of the Center · CHARIS

Marco Luppi

Coordinator, ICTS · Coordinator, CHARIS

Sophia University Institute

Marco Luppi is a researcher at the Sophia University Institute and Coordinator of the International Center for Transdisciplinary Studies, where he sits on the Council Board alongside the Director and the representatives of the founding universities.

He coordinates CHARIS, the Research Group on charismatic processes, institutions and social transformation, which examines charisma not as a theological or personal quality but as a historical and social process through which a founding experience generates enduring communities and institutions. The Group works across contemporary history, historical sociology, theology and organisational studies, with the Focolare Movement as one of its principal reference cases.

Research interests

  • Contemporary History
  • Historical Sociology
  • Charismatic Processes
  • Institutionalisation
  • Cultural Transformation
