FRATERNITAS

Andrés Calderón Ramos

Researcher

Sophia University Institute

Andrés Calderón Ramos is a researcher at the Sophia University Institute and a member of FRATERNITAS, the Research Group on fraternity, politics and social theory.

FRATERNITAS investigates relational categories — fraternity, solidarity, recognition — not as moral aspirations but as analytical tools capable of illuminating the historical development of modern institutions, the dynamics of democratic participation and the conditions of social cohesion.

Research interests

  • Relational Political Theory
  • Fraternity
  • Democracy
  • Recognition
  • Social Cohesion
  • Comparative Political Thought

Research structures
