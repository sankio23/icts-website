Emanuele Pili

Researcher

University of Perugia

Emanuele Pili is a member of CeC, the Research Unit on Christianity and Culture, which he joins from the University of Perugia — one of the Unit's partner institutions.

The Unit's work spans the genealogy of the modern age, personalism, the relationship between Christian anthropology and democracy, the theological-political problem, and Christian philosophies of peace.

Research interests

  • Philosophy
  • Personalism
  • Political Theology
  • Philosophies of Peace
