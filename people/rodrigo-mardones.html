Rodrigo Mardones

Full Professor of Political Science and Public Policy

Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile

Rodrigo Mardones is Full Professor of Political Science and Public Policy at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile and a member of the Scientific Committee of ICTS.

The Scientific Committee guarantees the scientific quality of the Center's research programmes and evaluates its outputs. The Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile is also a partner institution of the FRATERNITAS Research Group.

Research interests

  • Political Science
  • Public Policy
  • Comparative Politics
  • Democratic Studies
