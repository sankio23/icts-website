PDM

Leidy Vargas

Coordinator, PDM

Universidad de La Sabana

Leidy Vargas coordinates PDM, the Research Group on peace, diplomacy and conflict mediation, from the Universidad de La Sabana in Colombia.

PDM investigates peace as a dynamic process of conflict transformation, integrating three complementary dimensions — normative legitimacy, institutional design and relational reconciliation — and connecting academic research with public policy and field practice. The Group's signature initiatives include the ICTS Peace Forum.

Research interests

  • Conflict Transformation
  • Peacebuilding
  • Mediation
  • International Law
  • Political Reconciliation
  • Relational Governance
