Research Group

PDM

Peace, Diplomacy and Conflict Mediation

Conflict transformation, peacebuilding and normative integration

Violent conflicts today involve political, social, cultural, religious and institutional dimensions that legal agreements and diplomatic negotiation alone cannot address. Durable peace ultimately depends on the quality of the relationships that emerge after conflict.

PDM investigates peace as a dynamic process of conflict transformation, integrating three complementary dimensions — normative legitimacy, institutional design and relational reconciliation — and connecting academic research with public policy and field practice.

Central question

How can peace be constructed, negotiated and sustained by integrating legal, institutional and relational dimensions within processes of conflict transformation?
