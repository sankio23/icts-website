Research Group

FRATERNITAS

Fraternity, Politics and Social Theory

Relational political theory, democracy and social cohesion · Tabosa de Almeida University Center, ASCES-UNITA ‹to confirm›

Contemporary democracies face social fragmentation, political polarisation, declining trust and weakening civic bonds. While much political theory continues to focus on institutions, rights and procedures, these developments point to a deeper question: the quality of the relationships that sustain democratic life.

FRATERNITAS investigates relational categories — fraternity, solidarity, recognition — not as moral aspirations but as analytical tools capable of illuminating the historical development of modern institutions, the dynamics of democratic participation and the conditions of social cohesion.

Central question

How can relational categories contribute to rethinking democratic institutions, political participation and social cohesion in increasingly pluralistic and fragmented societies?
