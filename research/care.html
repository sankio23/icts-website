Research Group

CARE

Health, Care and Conceptions of the Human Person

Based at the Tabosa de Almeida University Center, ASCES-UNITA

Health and care are no longer understood solely in biomedical terms. Ageing populations, chronic illness, mental health, social inequalities and cultural pluralism reveal the limits of approaches focused exclusively on diagnosis and treatment.

CARE investigates health and care as historical, social and anthropological realities, starting from the conviction that every model of healthcare rests upon an implicit conception of the human person — one that shapes how vulnerability, suffering and wellbeing are understood.

Central question

How can more integral and relational conceptions of the human person contribute to shaping healthcare systems and practices that respond more effectively to the complexity of contemporary societies?

Core research axes

  • 01

    Conceptions of the human person and models of care

  • 02

    Health systems, public policies and social inequalities

  • 03

    Relational care, vulnerability and professional practice

  • 04

    Culture, spirituality and the experience of health
