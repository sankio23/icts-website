Research Group

CHARIS

Charismatic Processes, Institutions and Social Transformation

Based at the Sophia University Institute

Contemporary societies are marked by growing fragmentation and the weakening of traditional forms of belonging. At the same time, new communities, leadership models and collective initiatives continue to generate lasting cultural, social and institutional innovation that escapes conventional analytical frameworks.

CHARIS examines charisma not as a theological or personal quality but as a historical and social process through which a founding experience generates enduring communities and institutions. The Focolare Movement serves as one of its principal reference cases.

Central question

How do charismatic processes produce enduring historical transformations, and under what conditions do they remain capable of generating cultural, social and institutional innovation across successive generations?

Core research axes

  • 02

  • 03
