Programme

Where the Center intends to be at the end of its first four years.

The trajectory below is programmatic in character. Each milestone falls under the deliberation of the Council Board and the scientific evaluation of the Scientific Committee, and its realisation is bound to the partnerships the Center establishes along the way.

Trajectory

2026 – 2029.

  • 2026 Foundation
    • Constitution of the Center by its two founding universities.
    • Establishment of the Council Board, Advisory Council, Scientific Committee and Ethical Committee.
    • Three Research Units and four Research Groups constituted.
    • First international seminar series and inaugural publications.
  • 2027 Consolidation
    • First ICTS Peace Forum.
    • Permanent seminars on Relational Political Theory and on Christianity, Modernity and Politics.
    • First policy-oriented reports and applied ethical frameworks.
    • Opening of the doctoral and postdoctoral track.
  • 2028 Expansion
    • Festival of Charisms.
    • Constitution of new Research Groups from the Strategic Initiatives.
    • Joint European and Latin American funding applications.
    • Launch of the ICTS Fellowship Programme.
  • 2029 An international hub
    • International conference of the Center.
    • ICTS publication series with an international academic press.
    • International Network for Integral Care operational.
    • Permanent postdoctoral cohort across Units.

ICTS has chosen to publish this trajectory nonetheless. A research center that asks institutions and foundations to invest in its formation owes them a legible account of what it intends to build and on what horizon. Partners who join the Center in its founding years do not simply support an existing programme: they participate in determining which of these directions become institutionally real.

Outputs

What ICTS produces.

Six forms of output, common to every Research Unit and Group. Each structure additionally pursues its own signature initiatives, listed on its page.

  • 01

    Scholarly publication

    Peer-reviewed articles, book chapters, edited volumes, monographs and critical editions, published with international presses and journals across the Center's fields of inquiry.

  • 02

    International convening

    Workshops, conferences and permanent research seminars bringing together scholars from different disciplines, institutions and academic traditions.

  • 03

    Applied frameworks and policy

    Evidence-based reports, policy briefs and practical instruments — ethical guidelines, decision protocols, commitment frameworks — for public institutions, international organisations and professional practice.

  • 04

    Research networks

    Long-term partnerships and communities of practice connecting universities, research centers, public institutions and civil society organisations across continents.

  • 05

    Doctoral and postdoctoral formation

    Interdisciplinary supervision, advanced seminars, research mobility, executive education and international training opportunities integrated with the Units.

  • 06

    Public engagement

    Lectures, public dialogues, cultural events and third-mission initiatives contributing to informed debate on the questions the Center studies.

Annual reporting

A public account of the work.

From 2027 the Center will publish an annual research report documenting publications, events, networks and formation activities across all Units and Groups, together with a statement of progress against this trajectory.

Seminars, colloquia and public lectures are announced here as their dates are confirmed by the Research Units and Groups.
